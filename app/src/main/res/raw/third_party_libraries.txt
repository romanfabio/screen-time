Screen Time bundles these open-source libraries, all unmodified and all licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (full text below):

- AndroidX: Activity, Core, Lifecycle, Navigation, Room, SQLite, Startup, WorkManager, Profile Installer and their AndroidX dependencies
- Jetpack Compose: UI, Foundation, Material 3, Animation, Runtime, Graphics
- Kotlin standard library
- kotlinx.coroutines
- JSpecify annotations
- Guava listenablefuture 1.0 (empty marker artifact)
